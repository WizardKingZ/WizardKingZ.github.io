{{ site.name }} {{ site.name_zh }}

Johnew Zhang is a financial services and FinTech investor at CPP Investments. He focuses on sub-investment-grade private credit, including structured credit, corporate credit, platform equity, public long/short equities, and fund investments, across North America and Latin America. He has also sourced and evaluated esoteric, uncorrelated assets such as media rights and insurance, and contributed to the firm's data-driven investing initiatives and its market mapping of blockchain and crypto.

Before CPP Investments, he was an actuary in property and casualty insurance, working in reserving, product and pricing, and advanced analytics. He also has experience in mobile software development.
