About

{{ site.name }} {{ site.name_zh }}

Johnew Zhang has spent seven years at a public pension fund, investing directly in financial services and fintech across private credit and public equities. His focus is asset-based finance (ABF), financial institutions (FIG), and complex situations. He has also contributed to data-driven investing initiatives and to market mapping of blockchain and crypto.

He trained in mathematics and actuarial science at the University of Waterloo and financial economics at Columbia, and began his career as a property and casualty actuary. That path shapes how he invests: understand how a system works, price its risks, then design the structure around them. Most of what he writes here comes back to that idea.
