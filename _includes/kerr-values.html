Motto

Joy, Competition, Mindfulness, Compassion

Steve Kerr built the Golden State Warriors' culture around these four values. The questions and practices are how I read them, on a court or anywhere else.

01

Joy

Enjoying the process rather than chasing only outcomes.

Why do I love doing this?

  • Appreciating the privilege of competing
  • Celebrating teammates' success
  • Staying curious and creative
  • Keeping a sense of play, even under pressure
02

Competition

A commitment to excellence.

How can I become my best?

  • Preparing thoroughly
  • Embracing difficult challenges
  • Holding myself accountable
  • Improving constantly
03

Mindfulness

Staying present.

What deserves my attention right now?

  • Paying attention to what is happening now
  • Not dwelling on mistakes
  • Not worrying about future outcomes
  • Responding instead of reacting
04

Compassion

Not lowering standards, but holding people to high standards while understanding that they are human.

How do I treat myself and others?

  • Listening before judging
  • Building trust
  • Treating people with dignity
  • Giving honest feedback while respecting the individual

Together they come down to psychological safety, honest communication, and long-term relationships.

Kerr's four values as reported by the Associated Press (2015), Greater Good (2017), and Harvard Law School (2022).
