Steve Kerr built the Golden State Warriors' culture around four values: joy, mindfulness, compassion, and competition. He has spoken about them publicly since at least 2015, and they held through four championships ([Associated Press via The Philippine Star, 2015](https://www.philstar.com/sports/2015/11/26/1526341/kerr-values-streakin-warriors-live-by/amp/); [Greater Good, 2017](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/article/item/three_greater_good_lessons_from_the_golden_state_warriors); [Harvard Law School, 2022](https://hls.harvard.edu/today/steve-kerr-holds-court-at-harvard-law)). The values are his. The questions and practices below are how I read them, and I find they apply to an investment team as much as to a basketball team.

<div class="kv">
  <section class="kv-card">
    <div class="kv-n">01</div>
    <h3>Joy</h3>
    <p class="kv-def">Enjoying the process rather than chasing only outcomes.</p>
    <p class="kv-q">Why do I love doing this?</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Appreciating the privilege of competing</li>
      <li>Celebrating teammates' success</li>
      <li>Staying curious and creative</li>
      <li>Keeping a sense of play, even under pressure</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="kv-card">
    <div class="kv-n">02</div>
    <h3>Competition</h3>
    <p class="kv-def">A commitment to excellence.</p>
    <p class="kv-q">How can I become my best?</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Preparing thoroughly</li>
      <li>Embracing difficult challenges</li>
      <li>Holding myself accountable</li>
      <li>Improving constantly</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="kv-card">
    <div class="kv-n">03</div>
    <h3>Mindfulness</h3>
    <p class="kv-def">Staying present.</p>
    <p class="kv-q">What deserves my attention right now?</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Paying attention to what is happening now</li>
      <li>Not dwelling on mistakes</li>
      <li>Not worrying about future outcomes</li>
      <li>Responding instead of reacting</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="kv-card">
    <div class="kv-n">04</div>
    <h3>Compassion</h3>
    <p class="kv-def">Not lowering standards, but holding people to high standards while understanding that they are human.</p>
    <p class="kv-q">How do I treat myself and others?</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Listening before judging</li>
      <li>Building trust</li>
      <li>Treating people with dignity</li>
      <li>Giving honest feedback while respecting the individual</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

Taken together, the four values come down to three things a team needs: psychological safety, honest communication, and long-term relationships.
