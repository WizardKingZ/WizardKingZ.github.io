A hybrid convolutional–recurrent sequence model that combines local amino-acid patterns with long-range context to predict Q8 secondary-structure labels, with ten recorded validation runs averaging 64.05% masked accuracy. The full project report, including the data, architecture, results, an interactive 3D structure viewer, and the code, is at [/protein-secondary-structure/](/protein-secondary-structure/).
